Your 48-Hour Assignment
Three moves — pick the one your lowest score points to.
1
Translate one recent win into impact language
Before your next 1:1, rewrite your last project update. Replace effort ("worked on X for Y hours") with outcomes ("X reduced incident time by 40%, freeing eight hours of engineering capacity per week"). Send that version.
2
Create one skip-level touchpoint this week
Identify one leader above your direct manager who doesn't know what you've delivered in the last 90 days. Find a legitimate reason to close that loop — a brief update, a relevant question, a forward on something useful to them.
3
Name one potential sponsor — then earn it
A mentor gives you advice. A sponsor says your name in rooms you're not in. Identify one leader who could be a sponsor. Think about what value you could give them first — the relationship moves on that exchange, not the ask.
