# The AI Communication Audit — a Claude skill

Run this on your real emails and Teams/Slack messages and get an honest read on how your writing actually lands with leadership: tone, soft-skill scores, the personality signals you're sending, and rewrites that keep your voice but lower the relationship risk you can't see.

## Install (60 seconds)

This is a Claude Code skill. To install it:

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Move the `ai-communication-audit` folder into your Claude skills directory:
   - **macOS / Linux:** `~/.claude/skills/ai-communication-audit/`
   - **Windows:** `%USERPROFILE%\.claude\skills\ai-communication-audit\`
3. Open Claude Code and type `/ai-communication-audit` — or say *"run the communication audit on my messages."*
4. Paste in 15–30 of your real sent messages when asked.

No upload to us, no account, no email. It runs locally inside your Claude, on text you choose to paste.

## Don't use Claude Code yet?

Open `SKILL.md`, copy the whole thing into any Claude chat, paste your messages, and say *"act as this skill and audit my communication."* Works the same. (It also runs in Perplexity if you connect Outlook/Teams — it'll pull your messages directly.)

## What you'll get

- A three-word "tonal label" — your communication fingerprint
- Six soft-skill scores (1–10) with an honest read on each
- Strength signals and risk signals, named plainly
- 3–5 specific behavioral adjustments
- Three scenario rewrites — your natural version vs. a version that lands

One rule: read the first report straight through without defending it. The resistance is the data.
