# The AI Career Scorecard — a Claude skill

An 18-question, 5-dimension read on whether AI is accelerating your career or just making you faster at being invisible. This isn't a static quiz you click through — it's an installable skill that walks you through the questions like a coach, scores you, and hands back a banded report with one specific move to make this week.

## What it measures
Five dimensions, 18 questions:
1. **AI Fluency** — do you actually understand the tools, or run on surface awareness?
2. **AI Visibility** — does leadership associate you with AI, or not even know you use it?
3. **AI Communication** — can you translate AI into language that makes executives act?
4. **AI Workflow Integration** — is AI embedded in your day or still a side experiment?
5. **AI Leadership Positioning** — are you driving AI adoption or watching from the sideline?

You come out as an **AI Leader**, **AI Adopter**, or **AI Spectator** — overall and per dimension — with a verdict and a Monday action for each.

## Install (about 60 seconds)

**If you use Claude Code:**
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Drop the `ai-career-scorecard` folder into `~/.claude/skills/`.
3. Restart Claude Code. Say *"run the AI career scorecard on me"* and it starts.

**If you use Claude in the browser or any chat:**
1. Open `SKILL.md`.
2. Paste it into a new Claude chat and say *"Act as this skill and run the scorecard on me."*
3. Answer the questions honestly — it scores as you go.

## How to use it
Don't overthink the answers — pick the option that's most honestly true, even when it's not flattering. The scorecard only works if it's honest. At the end you get your bands, your verdict for each dimension, and the single highest-leverage thing to do this week. Then ask Claude to help you actually *do* that first move — draft the skip-level update, write the 60-second pitch, document the workflow.
