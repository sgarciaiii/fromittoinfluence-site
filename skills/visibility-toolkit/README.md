# The Visibility Toolkit — a Claude skill

Five plays for being seen by the people who decide your career, in one skill. It diagnoses where your visibility is breaking down, then runs the right move: making your credit travel, staying visible week to week, arming your boss to advocate for you, or resetting how leadership sees you after a setback.

## Install (60 seconds)

This is a Claude Code skill. To install it:

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Move the `visibility-toolkit` folder into your Claude skills directory:
   - **macOS / Linux:** `~/.claude/skills/visibility-toolkit/`
   - **Windows:** `%USERPROFILE%\.claude\skills\visibility-toolkit\`
3. Open Claude Code and type `/visibility-toolkit` — or say *"run the visibility toolkit, here's my situation…"*

No account, no email, no upload. It runs locally inside your Claude.

## Don't use Claude Code yet?

Open `SKILL.md`, copy it into any Claude chat, and say *"act as this skill — here's what I'm dealing with."* Same result.

## What's inside

- **The Promotion Gap Diagnostic** — 10 questions, scored, tells you which gap is holding you back
- **The Credit Moves** — four moves so your name travels with your work
- **The Weekly Visibility System** — a 5-minute Monday habit that keeps you on the radar
- **The Champion Brief** — what to send your boss 24 hours before a 1:1 so they can go to bat for you
- **The Strategic Assignment Playbook** — four moves to reset how leadership sees you after you've been overlooked

Not sure where to start? Run the diagnostic — it points you to the play you need.
